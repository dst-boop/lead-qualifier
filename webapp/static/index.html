Build a list from ZoomInfo

Standalone mode. Scoring, calling, email, invites and export all work here. Running a ZoomInfo search from this page needs a token — the line under the buttons says where you stand.

Tickers, not company names — ZoomInfo resolves a ticker to one company, where a name match spreads across subsidiaries. One quirk: BA is Boeing on the NYSE but ZoomInfo has resolved it to BAE Systems; check the employer column on the first page and swap to the company name if it came back wrong.

Min tenure sets positionStartDateMax — it asks for people who started before that date, which is the opposite of the Rollover search. Nobody is filtered on age here: ZoomInfo has no age field, so age is worked out after the pull and shown with its basis.

Equitable pre-approval is required before any SCS outreach goes out, and SCS+ material must carry the prospectus language. The app does not check either — that is on you.

Money in motion

Employers who have filed a WARN notice — a mass layoff or closure, with a legally required date — matched to the size of their retirement plan from the Form 5500 they file with the Department of Labor. Both are public. Dollars in motion is the plan-wide average balance times the number of workers leaving: an order of magnitude for ranking, not a forecast.

CSV template

Any export works — the importer matches columns by name, so a ZoomInfo, Apollo or CRM file usually needs no editing at all. This is the shape to aim for when you are building a list by hand, and it shows what each column is worth.

Header names do not have to match exactly. First Name, first_name and FIRSTNAME all land in the same place. Extra columns are ignored rather than rejected, and a single Name column is split into first and last on import.

Having someone else build the list

If you are pointing a research tool at this — Do Browser, Claude, an assistant — copy the prompt below rather than describing the columns yourself.
