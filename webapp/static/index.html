Upload a ZoomInfo CSV export and every contact is scored and sorted into four tiers. All processing happens right here in your browser — the file is never uploaded to any server.

Drop your CSV here, or click to choose a file Auto-detects ZoomInfo column names like “First Name”, “Direct Phone Number”, “Email Address”, “Person State” — and skips junk header rows.

Scoring: age inferred from graduation year (ideal 50–65) · years of experience (28–43) · senior job titles · recent job change after a long prior tenure (orphaned 401k signal) · retirement intent · phone/email on file. Frequent job-hoppers are deprioritized; Connecticut and Massachusetts contacts are excluded.
